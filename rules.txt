Rules
---

1. The game can be played with 4, 6, or 8 cards
2. The cards in the game should be as follows (with images):
  - Queen of Hearts
  - Queen of Spades
  - Queen of Clubs
  - Queen of Diamonds
  ---------------- 4 cards
  - King of Hearts
  - King of Spades
  ---------------- 6 cards
  - King of Clubs
  - King of Diamonds
  ---------------- 8 cards
3. When player makes a selection:
  - One chance will be deducted
  - If no chances remain, the result will be displayed
  - If chances remain, the game continues
4. If there is a time limit:
  - When time expires, rule #3 will be applied
5. Settings (changes will be active after saving):
  - Number of game cards (4 - 6 - 8)
    - With 4 cards: maximum 1 selection chance
    - With 6 cards: maximum 2 selection chances
    - With 8 cards: maximum 3 selection chances
  - Winning card (default: Queen of Hearts) (winning card can change based on number of cards)
  - Waiting time (can be between 5-120 seconds)
  - Time limit toggle setting. If enabled, time limit will be applied
  - Reset timer after each selection setting. If enabled, timer will restart after each selection if chances remain
6. At the start, cards will show their back face and will flip to front face when selected
7. If player loses, all cards will be revealed. If player wins, no need to reveal all cards
8. You cannot flip/unflip an already opened card
9. Cards will be randomly distributed each time the game starts
